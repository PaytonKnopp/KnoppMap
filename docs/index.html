Welcome to Knopp Map

A map of the quarter, its trails and photos. Here's how it works.

Getting around

👆
Move the mapDrag with one finger. Pinch, or use the + and − buttons, to zoom.
🏠
Lost?Tap Home at the bottom to jump back to the whole property.
🧭
Where am I?Tap Me when you're at the quarter to see yourself on the map. The blue beam shows which way you're facing.
🚶
Take me thereOpen a place and tap Take me there. A blue line leads you along the trails, like Google Maps.
↕️
Bigger or smaller panelsOn a phone, drag the top of any panel up or down to see more map or more information.

Places, trails & photos

📍
Named placesTap a round picture, like the house or cabin, to see its photos and story.
5
Photo groupsThe number is how many photos are there. Zoom in and they spread apart; tap one to open it.
🖼️
Looking at photosPinch or double-tap to zoom, swipe for the next one, and tap Show on map to see where it was taken.
〰️
TrailsTap any trail line for its length, hills and photos along the way.
▶️
TourA slideshow walk around the quarter, one stop at a time.

More to try

🔍
SearchFind any place or trail by name (top right). Small spelling slips are fine.
📏
MeasureTap the ruler (top right), then tap points on the map to measure the distance. Tap ✕ when you're done.
🗺️
LegendTap the folded map (top right) to see what the lines and pins mean.
🌦️
WeatherOptions → Weather shows live rain and snow radar and what's falling at the farm.
📴
No signal at the quarter?At home on Wi-Fi, tap Options → Print & offline → Save everything once.
⚙️
OptionsChange the look, map style, text size, feet or metres and more. Reset puts it all back.

You can open these tips again anytime with 💡 Tips in the top right.
