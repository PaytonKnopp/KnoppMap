How to use the editor

The editor is a safe copy of the map. Try anything you like: Undo takes back any step, and the family map stays exactly as it is until you send your edits to Claude.

1 · Go through the places

  • Pick a place from the list on the left, or click its pin on the map.
  • Check its name, icon and story, then look over its photos.
  • When it's right, press ✓ Looks good: next place. That ticks it off and opens the next place.
  • The Not checked filter shows only what's left. The bar at the top of the list shows how far along you are.
  • Ticks are only for you: they help you keep your place and never change the map.

2 · Check the photos

  • Click a photo to open it big. Use ← → to go through them all.
  • While it's open you can write a caption, make it the cover, hide it, move it earlier or later, or send it to another place.
  • A small map beside the photo shows where it was taken compared with the place's pin.
  • ⚠ far on a photo means it was taken a long way from its place's pin, so check that it belongs there.

3 · Hide the ones you don't want

  • 🙈 on a photo (or H in the big view) hides it from the family map.
  • Hidden photos stay here, greyed out, so you can bring them back with 👁.
  • Nothing is ever deleted: the original stays safe in the photos folder.

4 · Order, cover and captions

  • Drag a photo to change its order. The family map shows them in this order.
  • Sort by time puts them back in the order they were taken.
  • ⭐ makes a photo the cover (the big picture on the place's card).
  • A caption shows under the photo in the family map's viewer, and search finds it. A title replaces the place name as that photo's heading.

5 · Move photos between places

  • Tick the ☐ in a photo's corner to select it. Shift-click selects a whole run.
  • Then use the blue bar: Move to…, New place from these, Hide or Show.
  • You can also drag a photo onto a place in the list on the left.
  • Merge (bottom of a place) folds a whole place into another. Any tour stop follows it.

6 · Pins and the map

  • The open place's pin is big. Its photos show as numbered dots where each was taken.
  • Drag the pin to put the place exactly where it should be. Put pin back returns it to the middle of its photos.
  • Every photo on the map shows all the photos as small dots, which helps you spot one filed under the wrong place. Click a dot to open it.
  • Hover a photo to light up its dot, or hover a dot to light up the photo.

7 · The tour

  • The Tour tab lists the stops in order. Change a stop's text, pick a different place, move it up or down, add or remove stops.
  • A stop with no text uses the place's story.

8 · Send your edits to Claude

  • The Changes tab lists everything you've changed, in plain words.
  • Press ⬇ Download edits. You get one file, knopp-map-edits-….json.
  • In a Claude chat for this project, attach that file and say "Apply my map edits." Claude checks it, rebuilds the map and publishes it.
  • Your edits are saved in this browser as you go, so you can stop and come back later on the same computer and browser. To carry on somewhere else, download the file and use Load an edits file on the other device.
